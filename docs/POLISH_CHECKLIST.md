# Visual and audio polish checklist

Use a quiet listening level and verify the following before approving a Preview:

- [ ] Audio starts only after a browser interaction and never blocks gameplay.
- [ ] Ambience is quiet, seamless in character, and limited to active dungeon routes.
- [ ] Successful steps are quiet and subtly varied; turns produce no step.
- [ ] Rapid movement does not create uncontrolled footstep overlap.
- [ ] Wall bumps differ from footsteps.
- [ ] Rat alert and telegraph cues remain restrained and useful with multiple Rats.
- [ ] Sword swing and hit are distinct.
- [ ] Normal and perfect blocks are distinct without a painful level jump.
- [ ] Rune, Fountain, Cache, Resonance, exit, and damage cues are recognizable.
- [ ] Mute and all three volume sliders apply immediately and persist after refresh.
- [ ] Full, Reduced, and Off visual effects are meaningfully different.
- [ ] Reduced motion removes shake and unnecessary animation.
- [ ] Floor, walls, void, entities, hazards, interactions, and exits remain readable.
- [ ] Gameplay is understandable with audio muted and effects Off.
- [ ] Home and player navigation remain concise and use one primary action per section.
- [ ] Research Data, Topology diagnostics, and Model Lab technical detail are collapsed by default.
- [ ] Model Lab remains clearly marked DEVELOPMENT, SYNTHETIC, and SHADOW ONLY.
- [ ] Keyboard focus, skip link, tabs/details, dialogs, and controls remain operable.
- [ ] Layouts work at 375, 768, and 1440 px without primary horizontal scrolling.
- [ ] No console warning/error appears and no gameplay timing or deterministic result changes.
