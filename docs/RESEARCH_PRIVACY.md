# Research privacy and local data control

Research Mode is opt-in and browser-local. It does not automatically upload records, use an analytics SDK, create an account, fingerprint a device, or intentionally collect names, email, location, IP address, login IDs, or required free text.

Session IDs are pseudonymous record keys, not anonymous identities. Optional participant codes may be linked to people through a separate researcher-held list. Timestamps, behavior patterns, session structure, and shared codes can become identifying in combination. The interface therefore does not promise anonymity.

Data remains under the browser origin until the player manually exports it. Browser settings, storage limits, private-browsing behavior, or external cleanup may remove local records. An exported file leaves browser-local control when it is shared; deleting browser data cannot delete copies already shared. Storage, access, retention, and deletion of received exports become the researcher’s responsibility.

Pilot and Official records are explicitly labeled. Official summaries and analysis exclude Pilot data by default, and combining them requires an explicit toggle or flag.

Research deletion controls affect only research namespaces. They do not delete the normal active run, History, best records, permanent profile, settings, or character selection.
